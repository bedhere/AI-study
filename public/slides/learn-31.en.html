Ask AI one practice question at a time

Use the AI tool you normally work with to check your understanding of a supplied learning source.

Try these three steps

  1. Provide the material and your learning goal.
  2. Request one question at a time and answer before seeing the explanation.
  3. Check the explanation against the source and record mistakes.

A prompt to practise with

Quiz me using this material: [paste source]. Ask one question, wait for my answer, then explain with evidence. Do not reveal the answer first.

Use your own AI tool. Chat, selection-based questions and cloud note memory are not connected in this school.
