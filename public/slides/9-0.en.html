From a business task to a deliverable AI tool

A fictional content assistant illustrates the engineering decisions between calling a model and delivering reliable results. It is not a product already launched by Fangpian.

Start with a real task

Collect material for a short-video idea, create a script draft and ask a colleague to verify the facts and final wording. Define inputs, outputs and acceptance criteria first.

Explore eight capabilities as needed

  • Visual generation: references, prompts and consistency checks.
  • Tool loops: bounded operations with verifiable results.
  • Context: preserve the goal and remove stale information.
  • Memory: retain confirmed facts with traceable sources.
  • Prompts: separate role, task, constraints and format.
  • Collaboration: define what tools do and what people decide.
  • Permissions and observability: limit actions and record failures and costs.
  • MCP: distinguish consuming tools from exposing capabilities.

Complete a small end-to-end task

Map material input, AI drafting, human verification and delivery. Make one step work reliably before expanding.
