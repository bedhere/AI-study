Bring verified notes into the next task

An AI conversation may not remember your notes. Supply relevant notes yourself when starting a new task.

Try these three steps

  1. Record conclusions, sources, conditions and open questions separately.
  2. Paste the relevant notes into the next request.
  3. Ask which notes were used and check whether they are outdated.

A prompt to practise with

Verified notes and sources: [paste notes]. Current task: [task]. Cite relevant notes and do not assume unrelated or outdated information applies.

Use your own AI tool. Chat, selection-based questions and cloud note memory are not connected in this school.
