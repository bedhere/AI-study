Give AI the material and a clear question

Provide the source, question and constraints yourself. This lesson does not require a built-in school assistant.

Try these three steps

  1. Paste the relevant source, title and necessary context.
  2. Point out the sentence you are unsure about and the help you need.
  3. Ask for quotations from your source and for questions when information is missing.

A prompt to practise with

Source: [paste material]. My question: [specific sentence]. Explain only from this source, quote the evidence, and mark anything that needs checking.

Use your own AI tool. Chat, selection-based questions and cloud note memory are not connected in this school.
